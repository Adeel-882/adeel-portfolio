# Adeel — portfolio launch film

## Source and angle
- What: Raja Adeel Ahmed’s portfolio of AI automation, applications and connected business systems.
- For: businesses with repetitive operational work; shows how Adeel connects enquiries, bookings, reporting and daily routines.
- Distinction: actual working interfaces and workflows, combined with the portfolio’s spectral portrait and restrained visual identity.
- Strongest grounded claim: answers business enquiries, books appointments and sends confirmation emails; no invented ROI or testimonial.
- Visual hook: the actual spectral human beside “What are you still doing manually?”
- Real flow: dashboard overview → team view; voice workflow → calendar/email steps; fitness overview → meals/workouts.
- Tone: polished, cinematic, direct.
- Caption: “Less manual work. More room to move.”

## Identity
Exact palette from src/data/palette.json; Inter from the project’s font package; real project WebP screens and hero-spectral.webp. Screens are reused, not redrawn. All graphics are deterministic at time t. Original 120 BPM A-minor electronic score with soft percussion and tuned transition accents. No voiceover.

## Storyboard — 21 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps
1. 0–3.5s — Hook: “What are you still doing manually?” Spectral portrait, title reveal, then hold.
2. 3.5–8s — Dashboard: “Your company. One clear view.” Actual Company Command Center overview changes to the real team view with a restrained cursor cue.
3. 8–13s — Automation: “Answer. Book. Confirm.” Actual Voice & text bot workflow, with three sequential capability labels.
4. 13–17s — Application: “Built for everyday progress.” Three actual fitness screens enter in sequence and settle.
5. 17–21s — Outro: “Business, on autopilot.” Adeel’s name, service categories and supplied public email. Hold for contact readability.

Transitions dip through the dark palette; no overlapping busy layouts. Use the settled outro as the poster and replace frame zero with that poster. Original screenshots remain unchanged. A standalone renderer and intermediate assets live in work/.
