# Adeel — Business, on autopilot.

## Direction
An editorial portfolio for an AI automation developer and systems builder. Black negative space, large confident headings and a connected architectural object carry the identity. Thin rules and asymmetric columns connect the sections. Cobalt marks actions and internal pathways. Optical color is limited to a glass bridge and the supplied envelope.

## Typography
Locally bundled Inter Variable for display, Geist Variable for body, and Geist Mono Variable for metadata. Freely licensed Fontsource distributions; no remote runtime font dependency or commercial font downloads. Fluid headings and deliberate mobile line breaks.

## Hero
Original orthographic vector architecture: three thick hollow structures, recessed blue interiors, bridging corridors and two selective optical spans. It is one connected object in black space, not a video, orb, cube pile, city or conventional diagram. The geometry is static. GSAP moves separate wrappers for entrance, pointer response and scroll parallax.

## Content and work
The supplied résumé is the content source. Main projects: clinical wellness platform, dental voice agent and restaurant WhatsApp bot. Original typographic sculptures are identified as illustrations. Case studies explain problem, challenge, system, build and outcome without invented revenue, clinical validation or numerical automation savings.

Experience and education use actual roles and dates. Espace.homes and Elleven.pk marketing work supports the technical narrative. Toolkit lists only confirmed experience; course attendance is not converted into professional mastery. Phone/street address are unnecessary in this layout; public email and city provide contact context. LinkedIn is omitted until supplied.

## Motion
Short layered entrance, quiet native scrolling, small desktop-only parallax, section-specific reveals and a process line that follows scroll. No scroll hijack, bouncing, forced horizontal movement or custom cursor. Reduced-motion visitors see complete content without these effects.
